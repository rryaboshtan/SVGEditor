FAQ

Quick answers

How do I convert SVG to PNG?
Paste or upload SVG on the left — the PNG tab is already open. Check the raster preview, then click Download PNG. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded to a server.
Is the PNG transparent?
Yes. The download keeps transparency from the SVG (checkerboard in the PNG tab). Preview background swatches only change the live canvas — not the PNG file.
Is this SVG to PNG converter free?
Yes. Free SVG to PNG conversion, no account required, and no file upload to a backend.
What resolution is the PNG?
SVGEditor rasterizes at 2× the SVG width and height for sharper downloads on retina screens. Keep viewBox and explicit width/height on the SVG for predictable size.
